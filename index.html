Questions

Before you book

Can I book a home, a flight and a car together?

Yes. Choose a home, then add flights, cars, boats, carts or bikes in Your trip, or start from a package. We send one confirmation and you have one point of contact.

How do the 4-hour rentals work?

We deliver at the start time and pick up 4 hours later. You can switch to a full day if the next booking allows it.

How many people can go on a scenic flight?

Up to three passengers fly with the pilot. Total passenger and bag weight has to stay within the aircraft's limits, so we ask for approximate weights when you book.

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driver's license and proof of full-coverage insurance. Drivers must be 21 or older, or 25 or older for exotic cars. A refundable deposit is held on a card.

Can I drive the Jeeps and Bronco on the beach?

Beach driving isn't allowed in Destin or Walton County. We can point you to places to off-road nearby.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If weather cancels a flight or charter, we reschedule or refund it in full. Stays cancelled 30 or more days before check-in also get a full refund.

Peace of mind

How we keep you covered

Every home, car, boat and flight is checked before it reaches you. Here's what that means.

Cars and carts

  • Rental insurance on every vehicle, with your own coverage required as primary
  • Detailed and inspected between every rental
  • Golf carts are street-legal low-speed vehicles with lights, belts and tags
  • Roadside help through your concierge

Boats and jet skis

  • Yachts and charters run by licensed captains
  • Life jackets for every guest, children's sizes included
  • A safety briefing before every self-drive rental
  • Weather checks the morning of, with free rescheduling

Flights

  • Flown by a Certified Flight Instructor
  • A hangared aircraft maintained to an owner's standard
  • Weight and balance checked for every flight
  • If the weather isn't right, we reschedule or refund

Homes

  • Professionally cleaned and inspected before you arrive
  • Pool gates and smoke detectors checked
  • Damage protection offered at booking
  • A local team on call during your stay

These are our standards for the prototype. Insurance carriers, limits and requirements will be confirmed before launch.
